CoreTrace Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 25, 2025

At CoreTrace, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use the CoreTrace app.

1. Information We Collect

CoreTrace may collect the following types of data:

  • Health & Fitness Data: With your permission, we access HealthKit step data and allow you to log data such as meals, macros, weight, height, age, gender, and fasting periods.
  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymized usage patterns to help improve the app’s performance and user experience.
  • Device Info: We may gather non-identifying device information such as model and OS version to ensure compatibility and troubleshoot issues.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To power core features like meal logging, macro tracking, fasting timers, and weight tracking.
  • To personalize user goals and dashboards based on user-provided preferences.
  • To improve app stability and performance.

3. Data Storage & Security

  • Your data is stored locally on your device using secure methods such as UserDefaults.
  • No personal data is transmitted to external servers.
  • You can delete your data at any time by uninstalling the app.

4. Third-Party Services

  • We use Google AdMob to serve advertisements. Ads may be personalized based on device-level information. We request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission on iOS where applicable.
  • No personal data is sold or shared with advertisers or third parties.

5. User Controls

  • You can disable HealthKit access from the iOS Settings app.
  • You may opt-out of ad tracking through iOS privacy settings.
  • You can decline sharing features, and we will never share data without your consent.

6. Children's Privacy

CoreTrace is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Legal Compliance

  • GDPR (EU/UK): You may request to access, delete, or correct your data. Contact us at admin@exactrail.com.au.
  • CCPA (California): You may request information about data collected or request deletion. Email admin@exactrail.com.au.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may revise this policy. If major changes are made, we’ll update this page and the “Last Updated” date above.
